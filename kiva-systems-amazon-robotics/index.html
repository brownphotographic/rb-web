Rob Brown - Product Lead

Kiva Systems / Amazon Robotics

I was on the founding product team at Kiva Systems, building the robotic warehouse systems that Amazon acquired for $775M, which became the backbone of Amazon Robotics. I helped scale that technology across Amazon's fulfillment network: cutting costs, deploying new robotics systems, and increasing performance (1M+ bots deployed globally today). We succeeded with a low ego, high execution team, inspired and trusted to deliver. I led a team of product managers, mechanical engineers, and TPMs.

Product Manager, Kiva Systems (pre Amazon.com acquisition) 2010 – 2012

  • Requirements and human-machine interface design for pioneering warehouse robotics solution.
  • Direct engagement / boots on ground  with customers including Diapers.com, The Gap for product feedback.

Senior Technical Product Manager, Amazon Robotics 2012 – 2013

  • Design and development of Amazon’s first Kiva-enabled robotics fulfillment centers. Customer: Amazon Global Operations.

Product Management and TPM Team Lead, Automation & Robotics Advanced Programs (XB-15) 2013 – 2017

  • Next-gen warehouse robotics and automation including Kiva Systems robotics, AS&RS and robotic arms. 
  • Solution design & deployment of full scale building automation (1M sq ft), and hands-on support through peak operations.
